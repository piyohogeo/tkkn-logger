# Third-party assets

Tokkun '99 Logger is designed to interoperate with 『特訓'99 -君よ、男避けに避けろ-』 by びい氏. The original game can be obtained from the [official distribution page](https://bee.in.coocan.jp/tk); the author's [top page](https://bee.in.coocan.jp/) is also linked for attribution and thanks.

The game executable and its distribution archive are not included in this repository or in Tokkun '99 Logger releases.

The small PNG files under `data/template/` were derived from game screens and are used only as fixed templates for recognizing screen states and digits so that this logger can interoperate with the game. Rights in the game and the original visual expressions remain with their respective rights holders.
